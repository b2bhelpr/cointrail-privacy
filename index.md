## Privacy Policy

### Introduction

CoinTrail ("we," "our," or "us") is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is collected, used, and stored when you use the CoinTrail mobile application.

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

### 1. Data Ownership & Storage

CoinTrail is designed as a local-first application.

- All financial data, including transactions, accounts, reminders, and investments, is stored securely on your device using a local database.
- We do not operate external servers to store, process, or analyze your personal financial data.
- We do not have access to your data unless you explicitly choose to share it (e.g., by exporting a file and sending it to us for support).
- **Automatic Google Drive Backups:** When you have connected Google Drive in the App, it automatically uploads a copy of your database to your own private, hidden Drive folder about every 7 days — and only when your data has changed. These backups stay in your own Google Drive account (the developers never see them) and exist only for your recovery; you can also create a manual backup at any time from the backup menu.
- **Data Retention:** Your data is kept on your device for as long as you use the App — there is no server-side copy. Automatic Google Drive backups remain in your own Drive until you remove them; you can delete them at any time from the backup menu or from Drive itself.

You retain full ownership and control over your data. You can delete or modify your data directly within the App at any time. Uninstalling the App will result in the permanent deletion of all locally stored data unless you have created a backup.

### 2. Cloud Backup & Personal Stock Sheets (Google Drive)

The App includes optional features that utilize your personal Google Drive to enhance your experience.

**How it Works:**

- **Backup Tool:** Creates a copy of your local database in a hidden application-specific folder. This is accessible only to you and the App.
- **Stock Price Automation:** Creates a private Google Sheet in your Drive titled "CoinTrail Stock Prices". This sheet uses standard GOOGLEFINANCE formulas to fetch market data.

**Data Security:**

- **Private Access:** Both your backup and your price sheet are stored in your own Drive. We (the developers) never see or touch these files.
- **Scopes:** The App requests minimal scopes (drive.file and drive.appdata) to ensure it only interacts with the specific files it creates.
- **Encryption:** Data transmission is protected by Google's standard encryption protocols.

### 3. Data Export

You may choose to export your data for personal use or reporting purposes.

- **Formats:** The App allows exporting data to Excel (.xlsx) and PDF (.pdf) formats.
- **Destination:** Exported files are generated locally on your device. You choose where to save or share these files.
- **Responsibility:** Once exported, the security of these files is your responsibility.

### 4. Permissions

To provide its features, the App uses these Android permissions and capabilities:

- **Notifications:** Used solely for local reminders (bills, loans, transactions).
- **Exact alarms, reboot & vibration:** Used to fire reminders at their precise scheduled times, re-schedule them after a device restart, and alert you with vibration.
- **Files:** Used to save exported reports and to restore backup files you choose — through the system file picker, without broad storage access.
- **Google Sign-In (Internet):** Optional — authenticates with Google Drive for backup and automated stock price sheet management. When connected, the App can see and display your Google account email address in the backup menu.

### 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

### 6. Contact & Requests

For privacy questions or to exercise your rights — access, correction, or deletion of your data — contact us at **b2bhelp.r@gmail.com**.

- **Response time:** We aim to answer privacy requests within 30 days.
- **Where your data lives:** Your financial data is on your device and, if you enabled backups, in your own Google Drive. Deleting the App removes the local copy; Drive backups are removed by you from your Drive.
